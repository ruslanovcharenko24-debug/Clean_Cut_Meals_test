About this prototype

This is a student concept made for the MTU Brand Challenge 2026. It isn't an official Clean Cut Meals, Musgrave or SuperValu service, and nothing can be ordered here. No account, cookies or tracking are used.

Images and design

Pack images are from the SuperValu product pages, with only the empty white background cropped. The bowl printed on each pack is a serving suggestion. SuperValu, Uber Eats and Just Eat logos belong to their owners and only mark where each link goes. Colours follow the Clean Cut Meals Brand Book (June 2026).

The brand typeface Rink isn't available to us, so Lexend (SIL Open Font License) stands in for it.
