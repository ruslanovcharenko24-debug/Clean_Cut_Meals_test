Tonight’s dinner. Tomorrow’s lunch.

Ready meals for the evening shop: one for tonight, one for work tomorrow. For you, or someone you’re thinking of.

An example pair. Mix any two you like.

Thinking of someone?

Let them choose what they fancy.

How it works
  1. They get a link in a chat.
  2. They pick meals and how many.
  3. Their list comes back to you with pictures. You buy in store or online.

The range

30 products on SuperValu Online. Not every store stocks every product.
